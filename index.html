From Data
to Knowledge.

Research across data analytics, decision systems, and digital archaeology.

My research explores how data can be transformed into reliable evidence and actionable knowledge. Drawing on quantitative methods and information systems, I investigate questions across organizational decision-making, artificial intelligence, and archaeological research.

AN INTERDISCIPLINARY APPROACH

My work moves between methods and meaning, asking not only what data can reveal, but how we establish the reliability, context, and usefulness of what we know.

06 / ABOUT

A research path
across disciplines.

I am an Associate Professor of Management Information Systems at Marmara University in Istanbul. My academic background spans econometrics, statistics, quantitative methods, and archaeology.

Across these fields, I am interested in the ways research methods, data infrastructures, and disciplinary contexts influence the evidence we produce and the decisions we make.
