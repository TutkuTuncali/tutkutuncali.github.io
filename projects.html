PeVRsona — Personality-Aware Adaptive VR Narratives

University-supported research examining how behavioural signals from virtual-reality interaction can inform the adaptation of cultural-heritage narratives through machine learning. Principal investigator: Aydın Erden. Researchers: Tutku Tuncalı Yaman, Şebnem Sedef Çokay-Kepçe and Onur Birol. Planned duration: 18 months. Awarded support: TRY 828,000. The project is in research and development, not a completed application.

Funding source: Marmara University Scientific Research Projects Coordination Unit, Research University Support Programme (ADEP).
